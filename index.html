01

Push ingestion

Gmail/Outlook webhook fires the moment a message lands. Raw RFC 5322 headers + body pulled via API.

02

Auth check

SPF, DKIM, DMARC validated against the claimed sending domain.

03

Origin trace

First external hop resolved to GeoIP city/country/ISP, flagged for VPN/hosting providers.

04

Content scan

Subject and body run through the phishing/BEC classifier for urgency and impersonation cues.

05

Verdict & store

Score fused, saved to Postgres keyed by message ID, ready for the extension to fetch.

06

Chain anchor

Dossier fingerprint hashed and signed, written to the evidence-registry smart contract on a local Ganache testnet.

07

Attribution graph

Neo4j links sender, IP, ASN, and domain to prior cases and clusters them into a campaign.
